Bottom line

The reported "11% month-on-month improvement" is one volatile month (Feb→Mar, +11.03%), not a trend. Across all 7 months, recovery is flat — average change +0.3%, statistical trend test p = 0.89. No portfolio-mix shift, no denominator manipulation, and no statistically real channel-conversion difference were found to explain or hide any change. Recommendation: treat current performance as flat and invest for scale/efficiency, not to defend an improvement that isn't there — see memo for the ₹10 Cr recommendation.
